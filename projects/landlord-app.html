Landlord Mobile Application

Overview

Landlord is a mobile application built to help manage a 100+ unit rental portfolio. I lead a team of CS students on the project, and independently designed and maintain a locally-hosted server for the client that handles secure data storage and retrieval for the whole portfolio.

My Role

Beyond leading the team, I contributed directly to both the backend and frontend, working to keep the two in sync for a seamless integration and user experience. I also implemented the app's authentication and authorization system to protect tenant and property data and keep access restricted appropriately across the client's organization.
